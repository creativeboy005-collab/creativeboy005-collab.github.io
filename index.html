Why a custom PCB

Why make a custom PCB for your project?

A breadboard is great for testing. For submission, demo day and viva, a PCB makes your project look finished and work every time.

01

Looks professional in your viva

Examiners see a real product, not a tangle of jumper wires. A clean board with your project name on it makes a strong first impression.

02

No loose wires on demo day

Breadboard contacts loosen with travel and handling. Soldered tracks don't, so your project works when it matters most.

03

Smaller and neater

Everything sits on one compact board that fits inside a box or enclosure. It's easy to carry to college and set up in minutes.

04

Better sensor readings

Short tracks, a proper ground plane and filter capacitors cut noise, so sensors, audio and Wi-Fi modules give steadier results.

05

Safe for relays and motors

Tracks are sized for the current they carry, and power is kept away from signals. Thin jumper wires can heat up or drop voltage.

06

Easy to debug and explain

Every part is labelled on the board. You can point to each block during viva and trace any fault quickly.

07

One copy for every member

Manufacturers usually make at least 5 boards per order. Each group member can keep an identical working copy.

08

A real skill on your resume

A project with a custom PCB stands out in placements and internships for core electronics and embedded roles.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does a project take?

Ready projects take 3–5 days. Custom projects take 7–12 days, depending on the idea. If you need it sooner, tell us early.

How will I get my project?

Hand delivery locally, courier everywhere else (courier charges extra). Code Kits are sent instantly on WhatsApp or Google Drive.

What if the project doesn't work?

You see a demo video before delivery. If something goes wrong after delivery, we fix it over a video call or replace the part.

I don't know much electronics. Can I handle the viva?

Yes. Every line of code is commented, you get a setup video, and we walk you through the full project on a call before your viva.

Will another student have the same project?

We never give the same project to another group from your college. We can also add an extra feature so yours stands out.

We're a group of 3–4. How does pricing work?

You buy one Full Kit and split the price. Groups also get an extra discount, just ask on WhatsApp.

Can I get changes made?

Yes. Changes your guide asks for (an extra sensor, app or display) can be added. Bigger changes are quoted separately.

Do you manufacture the PCB too?

No. I only design it. You get the Gerber, BOM and pick & place files and order the PCB yourself from any manufacturer. I'll guide you through the upload.

I've never ordered a PCB. Can you help?

Yes. Ordering help is free with any PCB design I make, and ₹199 if you already have your own Gerber files. I guide you on the settings, check the preview with you and estimate the cost. You pay the manufacturer directly.

Which software do you use for PCB design?

EasyEDA. You also get the source file, so you or your guide can open and edit the design later.

How do I pay?

By UPI. 50% advance when you order, the remaining 50% after you see the demo video.
